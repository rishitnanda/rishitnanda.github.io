ABOUT_ME

Give me a complex conceptual problem, a messy process to automate with Python, or an event to run, and I'm in my element. I love figuring out the underlying logic of how things work—whether that's diving into the math and structure behind a system or writing clean scripts to make everyday tasks run efficiently. For me, coding is less about the syntax and more about the satisfaction of building something that just works.

But I don't just live behind a screen. I actually really enjoy managing people and organizing events. There's a completely different kind of problem-solving involved in getting a team on the same page and making an event actually happen in the real world. I love the fast pace of it and the challenge of pulling all the moving parts together into a successful experience.

When I'm offline, I'm usually keeping my reflexes sharp playing badminton or letting loose through dance. I'm also a massive fan of horror and supernatural movies—if it has a gripping plot, a heavy atmosphere, and a solid jump scare, I'm watching it. And when I just need to completely unwind, I'm probably catching up on the latest arc of One Piece.
